# Scoring

Use 0-5 for product traits, but never hide uncertainty.

## Trait weights
- Same-blade fit: 20%
- Same-side fit: 10%
- Speed/catapult fit vs reference: 15%
- Spin/grip: 15%
- Short game/control: 10%
- Counter/block: 10%
- Durability/QC: 10%
- Price/value: 10%

## Evidence confidence
- A: multiple same-blade/same-side reports
- B: similar blade + multinational agreement
- C: general evidence only
- D: sparse/conflicting/QC uncertainty

## Cost model
annual_cost = sheet_price * estimated_sheets_per_year
cost_per_hour = annual_cost / annual_playing_hours

Always show the assumptions used.
