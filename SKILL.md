# TT Rubber Scout

Reusable research workflow for finding table-tennis rubber alternatives using multilingual evidence, blade-specific fit, price, durability, and long-term cost.

## Inputs
- Reference rubber
- Blade
- Side: FH or BH
- Budget
- Tackiness preference
- Booster allowed: yes/no
- Purchase region

## Research Workflow
1. Analyze the reference rubber: speed, spin, hardness feel, tackiness, catapult, throw, dwell, short game, opening vs backspin, block, counter, weight, durability.
2. Discover candidates broadly. Do not lock onto prior candidates or brands.
3. Search in English, Japanese, and Chinese using local product names.
4. Prefer same-blade + same-side reports. For Viscaria, search Viscaria / VIS / ビスカリア / 蝴蝶王.
5. Separate manufacturer claims, measurements, single-user reports, and repeated community consensus.
6. Score evidence:
   - A: multiple direct reports on same blade and same side
   - B: same/similar blade plus multi-country agreement
   - C: strong rubber evidence but limited blade-specific data
   - D: sparse evidence, QC/counterfeit concerns, or conflicting reports
7. Compare: speed, spin, catapult, throw, hardness feel, dwell, short game, serve/receive, opening, counterloop, passive/active block, smash, weight, booster need, durability, QC, price.
8. Do not equate Chinese sponge degrees directly with ESN degrees.
9. Calculate real purchase cost, expected replacement cycle, annual cost, and cost per playing hour.
10. Final output categories:
   - Closest replacement
   - Best value
   - Best performance
   - Easiest transition
   - Alternative playing style
   - Experimental/dark-horse candidate
